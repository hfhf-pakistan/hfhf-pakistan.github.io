About us

Hope For Humanity Foundation is a welfare organization working for the people of our area in Pakistan.

We believe that a society becomes strong when its people take care of each other. Our work is simple: find families who need help and stand beside them, with dignity and without making a show of it.

Every year in Ramzan we deliver ration to needy families so that they can fast and celebrate Eid without worry. We help poor and deserving girls with their marriage, so that parents who cannot afford it are not left carrying the burden alone. We support orphan children in their studies, so that the loss of a parent does not end their dream of an education.

Now we are working on healthcare for our own area, with a medical center that will be open day and night.

Ramzan ration

Food packages for needy families during the blessed month of Ramzan.

Building a medical center so that treatment is available close to home.

Our project

The biggest step we are taking for our community.

Ahmad Medical Center, Chowk Phullarwan

Ahmad Medical Center will be the first medical center in our area. Today, when someone falls ill at night, families have to travel far to find a doctor. This center will change that. It will work 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so help is always close.

  • TimingOpen 24/7
